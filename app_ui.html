🔑 How to get your Kite enctoken
✕

Zerodha Kite enctoken expires daily after midnight. Follow these 4 quick steps:

  1. Open kite.zerodha.com in Google Chrome or Edge and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or right click → Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Click on the Application tab (top menu), then in the left sidebar expand Cookies and select https://kite.zerodha.com.
  4. Find enctoken in the table, double-click its value, copy it, and paste it into the enctoken field above.
